# Contributions to ReadAloud

ReadAloud is proprietary software. Public repository access does not mean the project accepts contributions under an open-source license.

## Permission required before contributing

- Contact **kweeziperez712@gmail.com** before preparing or submitting a contribution.
- Obtain explicit written approval from the project rights holder and agree the terms for any proposed contribution.
- Do not submit a pull request, patch, design, documentation contribution, or other copyrightable work until the rights holder has approved the contribution route in writing.
- The rights holder may require a separate contributor agreement defining ownership, assignment, or licensing of submitted work.
- Opening an issue, fork, or pull request is not approval and does not grant permission to use project code.

## Third-party code

Any approved contribution must identify third-party code, assets, fonts, datasets, and other materials and include their license/attribution requirements. Do not contribute material you do not have the right to license on the agreed terms.

## Contact

Send written permission requests to **kweeziperez712@gmail.com**. No contribution is accepted until the rights holder explicitly confirms acceptance and the applicable terms in writing.
